About

I believe the most consequential application of AI is in the life sciences, unlocking new ways to read and write the fundamental laws of nature. At the heart of true innovation is creative thought.

My work bridges deep-tech AI, frontier science, and strategy for real-world solutions. I'm currently leading product and strategy for a stealth neurotech startup, building Art Hack, and entering Fifty Years' 5050 Deep Tech cohort. Previously I have built AI tools at MIT's Lauffenburger Lab, worked for 2.5 years in Life Sciences Consulting at L.E.K. Consulting London, and joined Entrepreneurs First as a founder in residence. I completed degrees in Natural Sciences (University of Cambridge, undergrad) and AI innovation and application (Imperial College London, MSc).

I also love to explore creativity. I'm a classically trained oil painter and have been working as a graphic designer and artist for a decade with work for leading DJs and exhibitions across London, currently working on a portrait series of London musicians. This year I founded Art Hack, translating the structure for artists to prioritise creativity and help support the creative community at a time of cultural shift.
