About

I bring frontier AI and science to real-world health. I've worked across leading institutions in research (MIT's Lauffenburger Lab), life-sciences strategy (L.E.K. Consulting), and entrepreneurship (Entrepreneurs First), and I'm obsessed with personalised, preventative medicine for all. Most recently I've been working in computational biology for neurotech and female-health applications.

I've also been painting for as long as I can remember, classically trained in oil and life drawing, self-taught in graphics. My work has hung in galleries across London, and I've designed for some of the world's biggest DJs. Right now I'm painting a collection of London rappers in oil while setting up the second edition of Art Hack, the hackathon for artists.
